HOW TO ADD A POEM
-----------------
Everything above the first "@title:" line is ignored, so these notes stay private.

To publish a new poem, copy a block below and change the words:

    @title: Your poem title
    @date: July 2026
    @book: corona
    First line of the poem.
    Second line.

    A blank line makes a stanza break – it is kept exactly as you type it.

Rules:
  - "@title:" starts a new poem (this is required).
  - "@book:" picks which volume it goes in. Leave it out and it goes in කවිය.
  - "@track:" is optional – name a song and the page gets a play button.
  - "@date:" sets the order. Books read oldest first, so where a poem sits in
    this file does not matter – put new ones anywhere. Sinhala or English month
    names both work, and so does a plain 2024/09/10. Undated poems go last.
  - Save the file, commit, and it appears on the Journal page automatically.

THE THREE BOOKS
---------------
Write either the short name or the Sinhala title after "@book:":

    @book: kaviya    or  @book: කවිය                 poems, kept by hand
    @book: corona    or  @book: කොරෝනා කාලේ ආතල්      written through the lockdown
    @book: satahan   or  @book: සටහන්                notes from the studio

So to put a poem in the second book, add this one line to it:

    @book: corona
